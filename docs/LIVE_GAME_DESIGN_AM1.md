## AM1.1 — THE SEASON CLOCK (ruled)
═══════════════════════════════════════════
A season is 12 WEEKS (84 days), ONE CLOCK for both platforms
(free game and Web3 companion). A season equals one wave of
22 cards. This supersedes every 6-week or 42-day season
figure in LIVE_GAME_DESIGN.md.

## AM1.2 — PACING AUTHORITY TRANSFERS TO M1
═══════════════════════════════════════════
LIVE_GAME_DESIGN section 3.5's SEASON CHECK (42-day math:
"full wave ~6,600 Amsha, regular earns ~3,100") is MARKED
SUPERSEDED. The pacing authority is M1_DESIGN's 12-week
model: regular ~70 Amsha/day, dedicated ~90-100/day; one
faction wave in ~5 weeks dedicated, ~7 casual-regular;
dedicated players clear ~2 factions plus change per season.
The design intent transfers intact: nobody finishes early
and coasts, nobody runs dry mid-season — now proven against
the 84-day clock by M1's numbers, not 3.5's.
The 2-WEEK CEILING on any single card chase (3.5) STANDS —
it was never season-length-dependent.
