How to play

Keyboard: A / D or ← → move · W or ↑ jump · S or ↓ crouch · J light · K heavy · L special (needs a full meter) · Shift block (or hold back) · P or Esc pause · H show hitboxes.

Touch (landscape): put your left thumb anywhere on the left half — the stick appears there; push to move, up to jump, down to crouch. Right side: Light, Heavy, Special, Block.

Heavy is a high attack — crouch under it. Light must be blocked. Jump over a low projectile.
